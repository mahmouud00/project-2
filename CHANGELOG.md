# Changelog

All notable changes are listed here. Versions follow Semantic Versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH).

## [Unreleased]
### Added
- Sales guide instead of price lists (migration 0015): each line and each survey shows the company's cost of the job (materials after the supplier discount + waste + labour per m²) with two prices on it, the fair price (cost + 20 %) and the top price (cost + 30 %); margins and labour per m² are company settings. The salesperson types the price; a line without one stays unpriced and the quotation cannot be sent or accepted. A total after the discount under the fair total needs the sales manager's approval (`quotes.approve_price`, `POST /quotations/{id}/request-price-approval` and `/approve-price`); lowering it again needs another approval.
### Changed
- Offcuts are called «الفُضل», one offcut «فضلة» (was «البواقي» / «باقي») on every screen, the cut list and the docs.
### Removed
- Price lists (`/price-lists`, the «قوائم الأسعار» page, the customer's price list and the `pricelists.*` permissions). The tables stay for old quotations, whose line prices are kept even when recalculated.
- Formulas: a piece's name can be used in another piece's (or glass, accessory, check, drawing) expression and means that piece's length formula, exactly as if written in place (system unit, before rounding); pieces depending on each other in a circle are refused; the model editor lists piece names among the names available.
- Company prices are shown: materials carry `company_prices` (the current company's price, discount and price after discount per colour); the library list has a «سعر شركتك» column and the material screen shows and edits the price per colour.
### Fixed
- Model editor: the check's error messages were shown as empty red bars (the server sends plain text); they are shown again.
### Added
- Materials import from Excel (`POST /library/import-excel?apply=`, template `GET /library/import-excel/template`, "استيراد من إكسيل" tab in the library): colours, profile systems and materials in their colours with the supplier price, preview first and then all or nothing; existing codes are updated, nothing is deleted. MYTech imports into the shared library (reference prices); a company imports its own materials and its prices for library materials without changing them. Prices per bar and per kg (with a weight per metre) become prices per metre.
- Supplier discount (migration 0014): per material and colour (`discount_pct` in the company's material settings and the Excel column «خصم المورد %») or per profile system (company setting `supplier_discounts`); costs use the price after the discount.
- Offcuts (البواقي, migration 0013): every leftover of at least "أقل باقي يتشال" (400 mm unless the company set another value) is kept per profile and colour with its exact length when an order starts manufacturing; later orders cut from the offcuts in stock first (cutting optimiser `stock`), so fewer new bars are needed and bought; used offcuts are marked with the order; leftovers counted by hand can be added and any can be scrapped (`/offcuts`). Cut lists mark offcut bars with ♻.
- Sales confirmed by the accounts (migration 0012): a work order made from an accepted quotation waits for `POST /work-orders/{id}/approve-sale` (`sales.approve`) before it can be approved and made; "تأكيد المبيعات" tab in the accounts page. Orders already past the quotation stage count as confirmed.
- Notifications (`/notifications`): the bell in the program and a Windows / Android notification for each new one (Tauri notification plugin; the program asks the server every 15 s, on the factory network). Accounts: sales and purchases to confirm; sales: any change on customers, surveys, quotations, confirmations, payments, invoices, maintenance; production: confirmed sales and every work order stage; installations: technicians' jobs and their progress, maintenance requests; a technician is told about his own new job. Who gets them is a permission per section (`notify.accounts/sales/production/field`); new built-in roles "مدير مبيعات", "مدير تصنيع", "مدير تركيبات". Kept 90 days.
- HR (الموارد البشرية): employees with their job, department, branch, salary and leave balance, optionally linked to their user; bonuses and deductions on a month's salary; advances paid from a cashbox and taken back in monthly instalments; leave (annual, sick, unpaid deducted at salary ÷ 30 per day); monthly payroll per branch, paid from a cashbox with one payment per employee and closed after payment. Personal data reads are logged. New permissions `hr.read/create/write/delete/payroll` and a built-in "HR" role.
- The salesperson of every sale (`salesperson_id` on quotations, carried to the work order and the invoice; by default the employee linked to the user who sells) and each employee's sales in their profile.
- Purchase approval by the accounts: a new purchase order waits for `POST /purchase-orders/{id}/approve` (`purchasing.approve`) before it can be ordered from the supplier; "تأكيد المشتريات" tab in the accounts page.
- Company assets (no depreciation) and capital in the company settings; company profit month by month (sales − purchases received − salaries − expenses) and capital = opening capital + assets + accumulated profit (`/finance/profit`, `/finance/capital`, `finance.read`).
### Removed
- The warehouse module (warehouses, stock balances and movements, transfers) at the owner's request: goods are bought per order. Migration 0011 drops those tables with their data and the warehouse links of purchase and work orders; nothing else changes. `inventory.*` permissions and the unused built-in "warehouses" role are removed.
- Server installer: "mytech-service.exe Access is denied" on upgrade: while files are replaced the service's automatic restart is off (restored by setup), running programs are found through WMI (paths of service processes were sometimes unreadable), nothing may still run from the install folder before copying, and a still-locked service file is replaced at the next restart instead of stopping the installation.
- The same program on Android phones (`MYTech-Mobile-<v>.apk`, built by CI from the desktop code with Tauri): phone layout (side menu as a drawer, one-column forms, full-screen dialogs); technicians open on "مهامي" with the job cards, call / map buttons and the phone's own GPS (works with the factory's http server); map links and calls open through the opener plugin (map hosts and `tel:` only). Signed with the `ANDROID_KEYSTORE_B64` / `ANDROID_KEYSTORE_PASSWORD` secrets (alias `mytech`), or a temporary key until they are set.
- Moving to another server: `export --out file.zip` (database + pictures) and `import file.zip`; every backup also copies new pictures to `<backup dir>/media` (pictures were not in backups before). Steps in `docs/MOVE.md`.
- Field service (migration 0010): survey location as a Google Maps link (coordinates read from the link; QR code on the printed survey); maintenance requests per customer (`/maintenance`); technician jobs for surveys, installations and maintenance (`/field/jobs`) with on the way / arrived / done; GPS points from the technician's phone accepted only during a job, every read logged as personal-data access, erased after `LOCATION_RETENTION_DAYS`; the technicians' phone app served by the server at `/tech` (installable, same API as the desktop); new permissions `maintenance.*`, `field.read/manage/delete/self` and a built-in "technician" role.
- Work order progress: planned days per stage (`stage_durations_days` setting) give `progress_pct`, `expected_delivery` and `late` on every work order.
- Goods order from a work order: `GET /work-orders/{id}/material-needs` (whole bars after cutting optimisation, glass by type, each model's accessories, stock on hand) and purchase orders linked to the work order and its customer (`work_order_id`, `customer_id`).
- Capacity for 20-40 users on one server: database pool and request threads configurable (`DB_POOL_SIZE`, `DB_MAX_OVERFLOW`, `REQUEST_THREADS`); the server installer tunes PostgreSQL to the PC's memory.
- Server installer: the firewall rules for the MYTech port (TCP 8000) and discovery (UDP 47800) apply to every network profile, so other PCs reach the server even when Windows marks the factory Wi-Fi as "Public"; upgrades widen the old Private/Domain rules.
- Print any screen: a print button on every page of the desktop app sends what the page shows (its tables, details and totals) to `POST /documents/report`, which returns an A4 document with the company's logo and details to print or save as PDF. Allowed in read-only licence mode.
- Desktop: delete buttons (with confirmation) on customers, surveys, quotations, price lists, work orders, invoices (drafts; issued ones are cancelled), warehouses, transfers, suppliers, purchase orders, cashboxes and the library (systems, materials, models); add and delete buttons only show for users with that permission; the role editor groups permissions by section with delete in red.
- Separate add and delete permissions per section (`X.create`, `X.delete`; migration 0009 gives "add" to whoever could edit, "delete" to the built-in administrator only) and delete endpoints for customers, surveys, quotations, price lists, work orders, invoices, warehouses, transfers, suppliers, purchase orders, cashboxes and the library (systems, materials, models). Records with history are kept: anything already used is deactivated (`{"result": "deactivated"}`) or refused with a reason (`QUOTE_IN_USE`, `WO_IN_USE`, `INVOICE_ISSUED`, `PO_NOT_DRAFT`, `TRANSFER_SENT`, `MATERIAL_IN_USE`); money is corrected by reversing a transaction (`treasury.delete`) and an issued invoice is cancelled (`invoices.delete`).
- Model editor: the drawing (sketch) tab is hidden for models without a sketch; documents show the uploaded pictures.
- Arabic everywhere for factory users: engine and definition-check messages in Arabic (codes unchanged), every API error code has an Arabic message in the desktop app, built-in names shown as العرض / الارتفاع / عرض_الفتحة / ارتفاع_الفتحة / خلوص_العرض / خلوص_الارتفاع, constant and element names in Arabic (no English field left in the model editor), drawing types and openings in Arabic, a piece's role follows its Arabic element name. The demo library is written in Arabic and without helper values; the helper-values section only shows for models that already have them.
- Model editor: "copy the deductions of another model" (same system): pieces with their helper values and parameters, optionally glass, accessories, validations and drawing. Helper values (formulas) are now an optional section of the pieces tab instead of a tab of their own. Arabic inside code/formula text is shown joined.
- A picture of each model per colour (`color_images`, migration 0008): quotations, invoices, work orders and printed documents show the picture of the line's colour, else the main picture; exported in library packages. Model editor: "صورة لكل لون".
- Formulas in Arabic: Arabic digits, `٫` `،` `×` `÷`, the words و / أو / مش / صح / خطأ, Arabic function names (لو، أصغر، أكبر، تقريب، جدول، طول_القوس، ...) and built-in names (العرض، الارتفاع، عرض_الفتحة، ...). Each maps to the English form, so results are identical; names you define could already be Arabic. The model editor shows formulas right-to-left when they are Arabic.
- Half-circle arch (أرج) above a unit: when the total height is above the opening height, the arch is a separate fixed unit with its own frame, calculated with its library model (per unit or the company's `default_arch_model_id`) at width × (total − height). Its cost is part of the unit, it is a linked quotation line (`parent_line_id`, price inside the unit) and a work order item of its own (migration 0007).
- Formula language: geometry functions in exact decimals (`pi`, `sqrt`, `sin`, `cos`, `tan`, `asin`, `acos`, `atan`, `rad`, `deg`, `arc_length`, `segment_area`, `arc_radius`, `arc_angle`); glass panes can give their real area (`area_expr`) for shaped glass. Demo library: example arch model `AR1`.
- Survey sheet like the factory's paper form (migration 0005): survey `kind` (windows/doors or shutters), floor, technician; per unit: total height, sliding/hinged, colour as written, single/double glass and its colour, insect screen type, application; shutters: installation method and motor type. Edited as a table in the desktop app and printed in the same layout (`GET /surveys/{id}/print`, landscape).
- Suggested price per m² of a survey (`GET /surveys/{id}/pricing`): average of the survey from the real cost of each unit at its sizes and the price list; shown next to every survey on the customer page.
- Price per m² typed per quotation line (`price_per_m2`) or for all lines (`POST /quotations/{id}/price-per-m2`); each unit bills at least `min_billed_area_m2` (company setting, default 1 m²); the area is width × total height when the survey gives a total height (migration 0006 carries it to quotation lines), else width × height. Quotation screen and print columns: model number, width, height, qty, opening area, total area, model type and drawing, price per m², cost.
- Licence activation from the login screen (offline): public `GET/POST /system/activation` shows why the licence is not usable and the server fingerprint, and installs a signed licence while none is active (an active one is only replaced after login).
- Desktop program (`desktop/`, Tauri + React + TypeScript): Arabic RTL, light / dark / follow Windows, the company's logo and name on every page, LAN server discovery or manual address, every screen of the system (sales, production, inventory, purchasing, treasury, invoices with printing, library editor with element width/height pieces, reference tests and publishing, users and roles per branch, company branding, licence, backups, devices, audit and PII logs, price lists). Sizes in cm, width first, exact decimal conversion.
- Windows release build (`.github/workflows/windows-release.yml`): desktop installer (NSIS, offline WebView2), server installer (server compiled to native code with Nuitka, bundled PostgreSQL 16 as a local-only service, WinSW service, firewall rules, generated secrets, smoke-tested on the build machine), and the MYTech Licence Tool (compiled GUI: issue/verify licences, sign library packages and server updates, key management). Published as a GitHub pre-release.
- Server CLI: `version`, `fingerprint`, `backup`, `restore`, `update`; `init-db` and `create-company` read secrets from the environment (never the command line).
- `GET /purchase-orders/{id}`.
- Company branding: display name, phones, e-mail, website, address, tax number, commercial register, invoice footer, accent colour and logo (`POST/GET /company/logo`); public `GET /system/branding` for the login screen.
- Customer invoices (`/invoices`): created from a work order, a quotation or manual lines; model, image, drawing, sizes, colour, glass, price and discounts per line; invoice discount and tax; gap-free number on issue; cancel with a reason; cash receipts linked to invoices (migration 0004).
- Printable A4 documents (self-contained HTML, company logo and details on every page, sizes in cm, width first): invoice, quotation, cut list.
- Model pieces carry `element` (e.g. حلق، ضلفة، باكتة) and `side` (`width`/`height`) so each element's width and height deductions are shown together; passed through calculation results and cut lists.
- Company setting `length_unit` (default `cm`): the desktop app shows and accepts lengths in cm (the API stays in mm).
- UI mockups updated with the MYTech logo and colours, cm everywhere, width always before height.
- Trial environment: `docker compose up --build` (PostgreSQL + API + demo data: MYTech and DEMO accounts, an example library system clearly marked as not real, price lists, stock, customer, survey and quotation); `python -m app.cli demo` / `serve`; guide `docs/TRY.md`.
- "Authorize" button on `/api/docs` (OAuth2 password form, `POST /auth/token`, client_id = company code).

### Changed
- Server installer: PostgreSQL binaries are installed once (an upgrade never rewrites them); stop-services also ends a PostgreSQL left running without its service; the uninstaller stops everything before removing the services (it could leave postgres.exe running with its files locked). CI uninstalls with both services running and checks nothing is left.
- `GET /surveys` returns each survey's openings (the list showed 0 units).
- Server installer: stops the MYTech and PostgreSQL services before replacing files (upgrade over a running install failed with "Access is denied"), with `stop-services.ps1`: waits up to 2 minutes, falls back to `pg_ctl stop` and to ending leftover processes, logs to `logs\install.log`; tested in CI on running services.
- Licence files saved with a UTF-8 BOM (e.g. by Notepad) are accepted.
- Windows: the server exe is a console program (setup scripts wait for it); stopping the service also stops the server process (job object); the server installer turns off Inno RedirectionGuard (error 448).
- The vendor public key is a compiled-in constant (`app/licensing/vendor_key.py`) instead of a `.pem` file next to the program; a real key pair replaced the development key; private keys are stored encrypted.
- Windows machine fingerprint uses registry values only (no subprocess), so it is identical for the installer and the service and never depends on a timeout. Licences for the old formula must be reissued (none were issued).
- Customer statement bills issued invoices (with their services and discounts) instead of the work order amount.
- Survey opening sizes use `Decimal` instead of `float`.
- CORS allows the desktop program's origins by default.
- Compiled server layout: `app\<version>\mytech-server.exe`; the service launcher and the updater run the version in `current.json`.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-24
### Added
- Backend skeleton: FastAPI app, settings (`.env`), uniform error format, PII-redacting logging.
- PostgreSQL with three roles (app / system / owner), Alembic migrations, Row-Level Security on every tenant table.
- Companies, branches, roles (per-branch), users, devices, audit log, PII access log.
- JWT login with rotating refresh tokens (reuse detection), account lockout, change password.
- Optimistic locking (`version`) with `409 VERSION_CONFLICT`.
- Client/server version headers; `426 CLIENT_UPDATE_REQUIRED` for outdated devices.
- Licensing interface with offline signed-file (Ed25519, machine-bound, grace then read-only) and online subscription providers; device/user/branch limits; module gates; licence upload endpoint.
- Formula engine: own tokenizer/parser (no eval), Decimal arithmetic, whitelisted functions (min/max/round/ceil/floor/round_to/lookup/if...), dependency ordering with cycle detection, unit (mm/cm) handling, opening vs manufacturing size, calibration and overrides, generic sanity checks, drawing data.
- Golden test harness (`tests/golden`), any difference > 0.1 mm fails.
- Catalogue library authored inside the system: profile systems (UPVC / shutter / aluminium, mm or cm formulas, rounding, approved/trial), named constants, materials with colour variants (SKU per colour), product models with image and a full definition (parameters, formulas, cut pieces per material with their deduction formula, quantity and angles, glass, accessories, validations, drawing), vendor reference test cases.
- Publishing creates an immutable, hashed version of a system; publishing is refused if a definition is invalid or a reference test drifts > 0.1 mm. Calculations (`POST /engine/calculate`) use the published version and return the version id.
- Company calibration deltas and constant overrides, company material settings (price, internal code, hidden) kept outside library rows.
- Companies can create their own private systems; library rows are read-only for customers; library visibility follows licensed material types.
- Library update packages: vendor export (`POST /library/packages/export`), offline signing (`vendor_admin sign-package`), customer preview with a full change list (`/library/packages/preview`) and apply (`/library/packages/{id}/apply`); signature and file-hash verification, same ids at every customer, published versions never rewritten, removed items deactivated (never deleted), company calibration untouched. Online: only MYTech updates the shared library.
- Image upload (PNG/JPEG/WEBP, content-sniffed, 5 MB) and authenticated download.
- Company settings (tax, deposit %, quote validity/waste, saw kerf, bar trims, reusable offcut).
- Customers (PII-tagged, access logged), site surveys with measured openings (site size kept apart from manufacturing size).
- Price lists (end customer / dealer / contractor) with margin, labour per m², minimum price and per-model/system overrides (margin or price per m²); material cost resolution (company price → colour price → library reference).
- Quotations generated from a survey or lines: every line calculated with the published formulas (version stored), cost breakdown, totals, discount, tax, status workflow, explicit recalculation.
- Work orders from accepted quotations with frozen items, stage workflow (survey → quotation → approved/deposit → manufacturing → assembly → quality → delivered, or cancelled) and stage log; deposit check.
- Manufacturing outputs: cut list per SKU (length, count, angles, item references), bar optimisation (best-fit decreasing + improvement, kerf, trims, reusable offcuts, waste %), glass list, accessories, actual material cost, drawing data per item with model image; frozen once manufacturing starts.
- Inventory: warehouses per branch, immutable movement ledger with locked balances and average cost, adjustments, transfers, automatic issue of a work order's materials (all-or-nothing, no negative stock).
- Purchasing: suppliers, purchase orders, partial receiving into stock, last purchase price becomes the company cost.
- Treasury: cashboxes per branch, receipts/payments linked to work orders and purchase orders, reversals instead of edits, customer and supplier statements.
- Personal data (Law 151/2020): export of everything held about a customer/user/supplier, erasure by anonymisation (financial records kept), access-log query; log redaction fix for bearer tokens.
- Daily automatic backup (pg_dump, checksum sidecar, retention, single-runner lock), manual backup endpoint, verified restore that rebuilds the schema and re-grants roles.
- Live change events over WebSocket (`/api/v1/system/events`) fed by PostgreSQL NOTIFY from every audited change; no personal data in events.
- LAN discovery: UDP broadcast responder + mDNS service `_mytech._tcp`; client `discover()` helper.
- Safe updates: signed update files (`*.mtu`) with per-version install folders, mandatory pre-update backup, migrations with the new code, health check, automatic rollback (database restore + previous version) on any failure; `tools/release/build_update.py`.
- Windows deployment: launcher, WinSW service definition (auto start, restart on failure), install and update PowerShell scripts, firewall rules.
- Migration safety test (seed at old revisions, upgrade, compare data); CI workflow and pre-commit hook running the full test suite.
- Documentation: API.md, LIBRARY.md (authoring guide), INSTALL.md, UPDATE.md, ONLINE.md.
- `tools/vendor_admin`: key generation, licence issuing/inspection, company and subscription management.
